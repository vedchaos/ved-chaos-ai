# Quantitative Trading & Risk Management Engine

This skill equips the agent with mathematical, quantitative trading tools, rigorous position-sizing calculators, and zero-risk paper trading simulation.

## Core Directives & Truths
- Real professional trading is based on **mathematical probability and capital preservation**, never gambling or get-rich-quick claims.
- The 1% Rule: Never risk more than 1-2% of total account equity on any single trade setup.
- Risk-to-Reward (R:R): Prioritize setups with at least 1:2 or 1:3 R:R ratio.
- Paper Trading First: Backtest and test all strategies virtually before committing real capital.

## Tools Included
1. **`analyze_trading_technical_setup`**: Computes moving average crossovers (SMA), RSI momentum, support/resistance levels, and market trend bias.
2. **`calculate_position_risk_management`**: Computes exact position sizing and maximum loss limits based on account balance and entry/stop-loss distances.
3. **`execute_paper_trade`**: Logs virtual paper trades against a simulated $10,000 USD portfolio with zero risk.
